Best time to visit

November to March has the most pleasant weather. Summer is very hot, so plan indoor activities.

Dress code

Casual wear is fine in malls and beaches, but cover shoulders and knees in mosques and traditional areas.

Nol card

Buy a Nol card for metro, bus and tram in Dubai to save time and money.

Laws & etiquette

Public displays of affection, photographing people without permission and drinking outside licensed venues can cause problems.

Emergency numbers

Police 999 · Ambulance 998 · Fire 997.

Ramadan

Eating and drinking in public during daylight hours is discouraged. Many places adjust opening times.
