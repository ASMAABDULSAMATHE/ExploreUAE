International Flavours

The UAE is known for food from around the world, and that is part of the real experience. Over 200 nationalities live here, so a single street can offer a dozen cuisines. After trying the Emirati classics above, explore what the rest of the world cooks in the Emirates.

Fine dining

Chef-led restaurants

Dubai and Abu Dhabi host acclaimed restaurants with tasting menus and skyline views.

Indian

Curries & biryani

From quick dosa cafes to family restaurants, Indian food is part of everyday life.

Lebanese

Mezze & grills

Hummus, tabbouleh and charcoal grills shared with the whole table.

Filipino

Comfort dishes

Adobo, sinigang and sweet halo-halo are popular with the big Filipino community.
